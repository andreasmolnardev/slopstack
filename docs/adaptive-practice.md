# Adaptive practice

Starburst can build deterministic recall quizzes from a learner's knowledge library. A quiz can use explicitly selected concepts, concepts due for review, concepts with mastery at or below 60%, a mix of due and selected concepts, knowledge gaps, or the generated concepts for an owned course or module. Knowledge-gap practice starts with concepts that have no practice history, then adds concepts below 50% mastery. Individual selections are capped at 20; a full course or module can include up to 40 generated concepts.

`POST /api/learning/practice/quizzes` creates an owner-scoped quiz. Course and module modes resolve generated knowledge units from the learner-owned course curriculum and never trust client-supplied unit IDs. The response includes prompts, choices, and two progressive hints, while the answer key remains in the private `adaptive_practice_quizzes` collection. `POST /api/learning/practice/quizzes/{id}/submit` requires one choice per question, then atomically scores the quiz, records `adaptive_practice` evidence, updates mastery and review schedules, and marks the quiz submitted. Rejected or failed submissions do not partially change learner state. A missed definition is returned as remediation after submission.

The `/practice` route offers selected, due, weak, gap, mixed, course, and module modes. Quiz content comes from saved knowledge titles and definitions; no model service is required. Learners with at least 75% mastery and two successful repetitions get reverse-recall questions that identify a concept from its explanation; other concepts use definition recognition. Distractors use definitions or titles from other selected concepts when available and distinct fallback choices when there are too few. Adding clear definitions improves quiz quality. Prerequisite traversal and grammar- or reasoning-specific question types remain future work.
