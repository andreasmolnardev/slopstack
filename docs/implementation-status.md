## Known issues and remaining work

The deterministic course generator creates a short fixture lesson rather than a model-generated curriculum; it uses selected learner notes, while the live provider path plans an outline and generates lessons in two stages from selected owner-checked sources/concepts and profile context. The Audio Studio creates and validates source-grounded scripts in two provider stages, supports owner-scoped host/guest voices and turn playback, and stores a complete generated WAV on each episode. Audio archive transfer and knowledge-gap inputs remain incomplete. Language speaking practice uses Piper/Whisper with transcript-only English word matching; it does not evaluate pronunciation quality. There is no verified live LLM or speech integration or learner-history personalization. Captions use the saved profile default and timestamped segments when provided. Narration prepares an in-memory timeline on first playback; lesson narration assets are not persisted. Progress evidence is recorded per scene. Assessments currently support multiple choice, multiple select, true/false, numeric tolerance, exact short text/fill-in answers, normalized formula text, ordering, and matching. Rubric-based AI grading, diagram/widget/code/grammar/listening/spoken questions, mastery-based difficulty calibration, and prerequisite-driven remediation remain incomplete. Adaptive practice currently covers deterministic definition-recall quizzes from selected, due, weak, or mixed knowledge units. Offline assessments queue sanitized questions and answer submissions locally; final grading and evidence creation require an online authenticated sync. Production deployments still need a configured AI provider and Wyoming speech services. Robust scene schema validation, offline narration/media downloads, and broader end-to-end verification remain outstanding. The complete specification is not complete.
